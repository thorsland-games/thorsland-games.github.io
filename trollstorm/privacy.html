Trollstorm – Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Norsk versjon

Trollstorm is a game made by Thorsland Games (Norway). This policy explains what data is handled when you play.

In short

No accounts, no ads, no tracking and no purchases. We do not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts or anything else from your phone.

What stays on your device

Settings, level, missions and statistics are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

When you play online

  • Nickname: the name you choose is shown to other players in the match and on the weekly leaderboard.
  • Random ID: the game creates a random ID on your phone the first time. It is not linked to you or your device in any other way. It keeps your weekly points together if you change your name and lets us count how many people play each day.
  • Results: placement and eliminations are kept on the weekly leaderboard, which starts over every week.
  • Reports: if another player reports your nickname, the random ID, the name and the time are stored so offensive names can be removed. Offensive names are also replaced automatically.
  • IP address: used to connect you to the game server and may appear in technical server logs for up to 30 days.

The game servers are in Norway and the EU/EEA. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The game does not ask for personal data. We recommend choosing a nickname that is not your real name.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access and delete data about you. Uninstalling the app deletes everything on your device. To have leaderboard entries or reports about you removed from the server, e-mail games@thorsland.no. You may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Contact

E-mail: games@thorsland.no

Trollstorm – personvern

Trollstorm lages av Thorsland Games (Norge). Spillet har ingen konto, ingen reklame, ingen sporing og ingen kjøp.

Innstillinger, nivå, oppdrag og statistikk lagres bare på telefonen og slettes når du avinstallerer spillet.

Når du spiller på nett, vises kallenavnet ditt for andre spillere og på ukens toppliste. En tilfeldig ID laget på telefonen holder poengene dine samlet og brukes til å telle hvor mange som spiller. Resultater (plassering og forsteinede troll) ligger på ukens toppliste, som nullstilles hver uke. Blir navnet ditt rapportert, lagres ID, navn og tidspunkt så støtende navn kan fjernes. IP-adressen brukes til å koble deg til spillserveren og kan ligge i tekniske logger i opptil 30 dager. Serverne står i Norge og EU/EØS, og ingenting deles eller selges.

Du kan be om innsyn og sletting på games@thorsland.no, og du kan klage til Datatilsynet.
