Spinferno – Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Spinferno is a game made by Thorsland Games (Norway). This policy explains what data is handled when you play.

What the game itself stores

Your best score, number of games played and your settings (sound, vibration, steering) are stored only on your device. We do not have user accounts and we do not receive this data. Tilt steering reads the phone's motion sensor on the device only; it is never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The game is free and shows an occasional full-screen ad between rounds, served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC). To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information,
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic data.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent dialog the first time you open the game. You can change your choice at any time with the ⚙ button on the start screen. On iOS you can also allow or deny tracking in the system prompt and later in Settings → Privacy → Tracking. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Read how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy.

Children

Spinferno is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data and to object to processing. Since we do not hold any personal data about you ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. You may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Contact

Questions: use the contact e-mail shown on Spinferno's page in Google Play or the App Store.
